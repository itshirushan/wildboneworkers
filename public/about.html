Premium specimens in wholesale lots

The workshop behind the collection

Wild Bone Workers processes and packages genuine animal skulls and bone specimens for the oddity shops, dark artists, and collectors who build with them. Rather than selling one piece at a time, we prepare ready-to-use wholesale lots — so you spend less time sourcing individual pieces and more time creating.

Every specimen is cleaned by hand and inspected before it leaves the workshop, aiming for a consistent, natural finish across each batch.

Sourcing & ethics

All specimens are obtained legally and ethically, in line with Etsy's marketplace policies. We do not sell material from any species listed as threatened or endangered under the U.S. Endangered Species Act, or listed in Appendix I of CITES (the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora).

Wholesale & custom orders

We work with oddity shop owners, museum educators, artists, and collectors who need larger quantities. If you have a bulk request beyond what's listed, reach out through the shop and we'll do our best to accommodate it, depending on current inventory and sourcing schedules.

Frequently asked questions

Are your specimens ethically sourced?

Yes. Every item is legally and ethically obtained, and we never list species designated as threatened or endangered by the U.S. Endangered Species Act or CITES Appendix I.

Do you take custom or wholesale orders?

Yes — message the shop with what you're looking for and the quantity you need. Larger orders depend on current lab inventory and sourcing schedules.

Where can I see current stock and pricing?

The Shop page mirrors the live Etsy catalog, including current price and stock for every specimen.

Product listings, prices, and stock on this site are synced automatically from our Etsy shop via the Etsy Open API — nothing here is entered by hand or out of date with what's on Etsy.
