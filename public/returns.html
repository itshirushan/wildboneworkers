Returns & exchanges

Because every purchase is completed on Etsy, your return and exchange eligibility is the one shown on that specific Etsy listing at the time you buy, and any return or refund is handled through Etsy's order and buyer-protection system — not this site.

How to start a return

  1. Open your order in Etsy > Purchases.
  2. Select the order and choose "Problem with order," or message the shop directly.
  3. If the specimen arrived damaged, include a few photos — this speeds up resolution considerably.

Please note

Natural specimens vary piece to piece — minor differences in size, color, or bone density between photos and the item received are expected and are not typically treated as damage. If something looks genuinely wrong with your order, reach out and we'll work with you through Etsy.

Exact return windows and conditions can differ by listing — check the "Returns & exchanges" section on the specific Etsy listing you purchased for the terms that apply to your order.
