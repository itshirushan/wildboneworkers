Shipping

Every order placed from this site is completed on Etsy, so Etsy's checkout is what calculates final shipping cost, carrier, and delivery estimate for your address at checkout time — those numbers can shift with destination and order size, so we don't restate them here to avoid showing you anything out of date.

What to expect

  • Most listings ship free, as shown on the product page and at checkout.
  • Exact dispatch time and carrier are listed on each Etsy listing under "Shipping" and confirmed again at checkout.
  • Tracking, delivery estimates, and any customs information for international orders are provided by Etsy once your order ships.

Specimens & packaging

Bone and skull specimens are fragile. They're packed individually to limit movement in transit — if anything arrives damaged, contact us through Etsy Messages with photos as soon as possible so we can make it right.

For the shipping cost, carrier, and estimated delivery date on a specific specimen, open that listing on Etsy — the details there are always current, since checkout happens on Etsy.
