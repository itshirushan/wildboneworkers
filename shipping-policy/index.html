Before you order

Shipping Policy

WildBoneWorkers uses Etsy for checkout, shipping calculations and order communication.

Shipping details

Shipping cost, destination availability and estimated delivery dates are shown on the individual Etsy listing at checkout. Because these details can change by destination and carrier, the Etsy listing is the current source of truth.

International orders

International buyers should review the Etsy checkout information for their destination before purchasing. Government duties, taxes, tariffs or import fees may apply depending on the destination and are outside the control of WildBoneWorkers.

Packaging

Natural bone and specimen pieces are packed for transport. Fragile specimens can contain thin or delicate structures, so please handle them carefully after delivery.

Tracking & delivery

Where tracking is provided, tracking information is supplied through Etsy. Delivery estimates are estimates rather than guarantees and can change because of carrier delays, customs processing or local conditions.

Destination restrictions

Some natural-history specimens may have destination-specific wildlife or customs restrictions. Before ordering internationally, confirm that the item can legally be imported into your country and review any requirements shown by Etsy or your local authorities.
