Shipping and Return Policies

Shipping Policy

We are dedicated to getting your natural history specimens to you as safely and quickly as possible. Because we ship internationally, delivery times can vary.

  • Processing Time: Orders are typically processed and dispatched within the timeframe specified on the individual Etsy or eBay listing (usually 1-3 business days).
  • Packaging: Bone specimens are fragile. We take extreme care in packaging your items with ample padding to prevent damage during transit.
  • International Shipping & Customs (USA, UK, Canada): We ship to the USA, UK, and Canada regularly. Buyers are responsible for any customs and import taxes that may apply in their respective countries. We are not responsible for delays due to customs.

✅ Fully Licensed & Compliant

WildBoneWorkers holds all necessary licences for the international trade of animal osteology specimens. We comply with all applicable wildlife trade regulations, platform policies, and customs requirements. Free worldwide shipping is included on every order.

Return Policy

Your satisfaction is important to us. If you have an issue with your order, please contact us immediately.

  • Damaged Items: If your item arrives damaged despite our careful packaging, please contact us within 14 days of delivery with photos of the damaged item and the packaging.
  • Returns & Exchanges: We generally accept returns if you contact us within 14 days of delivery and ship the item back within 30 days of delivery.
  • Conditions of Return: Buyers are responsible for return shipping costs. If the item is not returned in its original condition, the buyer is responsible for any loss in value.
  • Platform Policies: Because all transactions are processed via Etsy and eBay, the official return process must be initiated through the respective platform's messaging and resolution center.
