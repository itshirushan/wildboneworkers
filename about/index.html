About the shop

Wild Bone Workers

A lightweight natural-history catalog for collectors, artists, educators, makers and curious minds.

Wild Bone Workers offers real animal skulls, skeletons, insect specimens, marine specimens and craft-grade bone supplies. This website is a lightweight catalog; Etsy remains the checkout platform for current listings.

Wholesale & Bulk Orders

The shop focuses on natural-history specimens and bulk craft supplies, including skulls, skeletons, insect specimens and marine specimens. Larger wholesale inquiries may be possible depending on current inventory; contact the shop through Etsy.

Natural Specimens

Natural specimens can vary in size, shape, color and surface detail. Review each Etsy listing for current photographs, measurements, availability and item-specific notes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I purchase?

Every product page links to its corresponding Etsy listing. Etsy handles checkout, payment, shipping information and order communication.

Are all specimens identical?

No. Each natural specimen is unique, and small variations are normal.
