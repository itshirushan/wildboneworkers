About the shop

Wild Bone Workers

A simple catalog for collectors, artists, educators and oddities shops looking for natural history specimens and bone supplies.

Wild Bone Workers offers real animal skulls, skeletons, insect specimens, marine specimens and craft-grade bone supplies. This website is a lightweight catalog, while Etsy remains the place for current listing information and checkout.

Wholesale & bulk orders

Wholesale and bulk-order inquiries are welcome when inventory allows. Contact Wild Bone Workers through Etsy to discuss larger quantities.

Natural specimens

Natural specimens can vary in size, shape, color and surface detail. Review each Etsy listing for current photos, dimensions, availability and item-specific notes.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I purchase?

Product pages link directly to the corresponding Etsy listing. Etsy handles checkout, shipping information and order communication.

Do you offer bulk quantities?

Some listings are already sold in bulk lots. Larger wholesale inquiries may be possible depending on inventory.

Are all specimens identical?

No. Natural specimens are individual pieces and may have small differences in size, shape or appearance.
