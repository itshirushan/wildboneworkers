About

Wild Bone Workers

Wild Bone Workers is a small specimen shop focused on natural history pieces, animal skulls, skeletons, insect specimens and bulk bone supplies for collectors, artists, educators and oddity shops.

A simple way to browse

This website is a fast, easy-to-search catalog. Etsy remains the checkout platform for current listings, shipping information and availability.

Wholesale & bulk orders

Wholesale and bulk-order inquiries are welcome when inventory allows. If you are an oddities shop owner, artist, educator or collector looking for larger quantities, please contact Wild Bone Workers through Etsy.

Responsible sourcing

For sourcing, species and international shipping questions, review the current Etsy listing before purchasing. Wildlife trade rules can vary by species and destination.
