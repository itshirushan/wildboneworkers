About WildBoneWorkers

Welcome to WildBoneWorkers, your premier destination for high-quality, authentic animal bone specimens and osteological decor.

We specialize in curating and preparing a wide variety of skulls, skeletons, and natural oddities. Whether you are an educator needing specimens for a biology class, a taxidermy artist looking for supplies, or a collector decorating a cabinet of curiosities, we have something unique for you.

Ethically Sourced

We understand the importance of respecting nature. That is why our primary focus is on providing ethically sourced specimens. Our bones are collected as natural by-products, salvage, or natural deaths. We believe in honoring the animal by preserving its natural beauty for educational and artistic purposes.

Professional Preparation

Cleaning bones is a meticulous process. We take pride in our preparation methods, ensuring that every skull and skeleton is properly macerated, degreased, and whitened (when applicable) to ensure a museum-quality finish that will last for years without degradation or odor.
